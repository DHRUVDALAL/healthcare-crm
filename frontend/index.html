About

Built for commission-driven recruitment

A staffing agency earns commission when a candidate gets hired - this CRM is designed around that outcome.

How the agency works

Hospitals share job openings and hiring requirements.
Your team sources applicants and shortlists candidates.
Hospitals interview and select the final hire.
Invoice is generated: salary × commission %.
