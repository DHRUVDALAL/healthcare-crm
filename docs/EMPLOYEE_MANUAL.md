# Recruiter & Employee Workspace Manual

**System Title**: HealthCRM - Enterprise Healthcare Recruitment ERP + CRM  

---

## 1. Recruiter Command Center

- **Today's Work Center**: Access 10 operational streams (Overdue Follow-ups, Interviews Today, Hospital Feedback Pending, New Candidate Assignments, Candidates Ready For Submission, Tasks Due Today, Personal Notes, Calendar Events, Upcoming Joinings, Offer Follow-ups).
- **7-Tier Work Queue**: Prioritize daily tasks from Urgent Follow-ups to Low Priority Activities.
- **One-Click Quick Actions**: Call Candidate, Schedule Interview, Upload Document, Move Pipeline Stage, and Generate Submission PDF package.
- **Recruiter Gamified Leaderboard**: Track monthly placements, revenue contribution, conversion rate, and performance badge level.
